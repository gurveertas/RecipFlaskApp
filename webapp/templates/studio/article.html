How to read this page — QA verdict & rewrites

Publish candidate = zero failed checks. Blocked = at least one FAIL — the detail line names the exact problem (a hallucinated figure, a copied phrase, a missing keyword). WARNs never block; they're for your judgement.

References
The [S#] sources the article actually cites — real pages found during research.
Fix selected
Tick the specific checks you want fixed and leave the rest. Aimed at just those, grounded in this article's own sources. Blocking issues (FAIL / mandatory) are pre-ticked; WARNs and advisories are not, since those are often a deliberate call. Use this first.
Anything not ticked is kept as written, and that is structural rather than a request: the draft is sent as numbered paragraphs and the AI returns a list of edits, so a paragraph it does not name is not rewritten — it is not in the response at all. Of the edits it does make, any that breaks a check which was passing is dropped. What cannot be dropped without undoing the fix is retried, and the whole attempt is discarded if it would leave the article unpublishable. A pass costs 1 AI call normally, up to 3 when a fix and a regression sit in the same sentences — the message afterwards says when that happened.
Rewrite one section
Pick an H2, optionally give an instruction (“add the exam cost”, “link to <url>”). One LLM call, ~10s, citations kept, QA re-runs. For edits no check has flagged.
Rewrite whole article
Full research + write + QA cycle (1-3 min). For structural problems, not small fixes.
REACS
A second, separate content-standard gate (mandatory + advisory "pillars", score ≥{{ REACS_THRESHOLD }}) — see the panel below. Publishing needs QA and REACS to pass.
Continue to Preview
Appears only when both QA and REACS pass. Preview shows the article in the live site's formatting before publishing.

What each QA check verifies: see the Guide.
