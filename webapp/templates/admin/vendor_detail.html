What each tab does
Priority
P1-P5 commercial priority enables generation and publishing. Research only keeps the vendor available as a factual source while blocking new commercial content.
Products
The vendor's product line and lifecycle status (current / legacy / end-of-life). Context the writer can draw on.
Certifications
Current or retired. Marking one retired makes it a QA tripwire: any article treating it as current gets a warning. Retirement year, replacement, and custom notes are all used in that warning.
Courses
Each belongs to a certification and needs a URL. Fed to the writer as real course names/durations and used for semantic entity coverage.
