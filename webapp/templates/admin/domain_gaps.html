How allowlist review works

Open-web retrieval records domains that appeared for a topic but were excluded by the source allowlist. Nothing listed here has been used in an article.

Seen
How often the domain appeared. Frequency is evidence to review, not a quality score.
Category
How retrieval should treat the source. Vendor sources can be attached to one vendor.
Tier
Tier 1 is authoritative; tier 2 is an established secondary source.
Approve
Adds the domain to the source allowlist for future research.
Ignore
Keeps the domain blocked and moves it out of the active queue.
Awaiting a decision

Review the latest result before allowing a domain into future article research.
